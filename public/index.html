Is my store the right size?
If you're on Shopify and doing roughly $20–50k a month, yes. Agencies are built for brands doing $1M or more a year; this is built for yours. Below $20k the list is usually too small for a monthly service to pay for itself, and I'll tell you if so. Above $50k, get in touch anyway.
What is the free email check?
Set the sliders below and send the form. I ask for read-only Klaviyo access, or screenshots if you'd rather. You get a short written review of where you are and what I'd fix first. No call, and it's yours to keep.
Where does 25% come from? Is it realistic for me?
It's a benchmark, not a promise. Shopify stores with the main flows running and campaigns going out regularly tend to get 25–35% of revenue from email. Smaller lists start lower and take time to get there. The free check tells you where you are now and what's realistic for your store.
What does it cost?
A fixed monthly fee, agreed in writing before we start. It depends on what's missing and how many campaigns you need, so I quote after the email check. If I don't think email can earn back the fee at your size, I'll say so instead of quoting.
How much of my time does it take?
About an hour at the start, to give me access and tell me about your products and customers. After that, a few minutes a week to approve emails before they go out.
Who will I work with?
Me, Augustinas. I plan the emails, write them, design and build them, and set them up in Klaviyo. There's no account manager and nothing is outsourced.
Is there a contract?
No. It's month to month with two weeks' notice. Everything I build stays in your Klaviyo account and it's yours.
Will the emails sound like my brand?
They should sound like you on a good day. Before I write anything I read your site, your reviews and the emails you've already sent. You approve every email before it goes out.
Can't I just write the emails with AI?
For a first draft, yes. But writing is the smaller part. Someone still has to decide who gets which email and when, build it so it looks right on a phone, keep the list clean, and notice when emails start going to spam.
My emails are going to spam. Can you fix that?
Usually, yes. I find out why, clean the list, and rebuild your sender reputation step by step. It takes a few weeks, not a day, and I keep watching it afterwards so it doesn't slip back.
How soon will I see a difference?
Flows first: welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase. They start earning as soon as they're live, usually within a few weeks. Campaigns build on that month by month. I'll tell you what to expect for your store after the check. I won't promise a number.
Do you do SMS?
Not as part of the standard service. Once your email is set up and working, SMS is available as an add-on.
